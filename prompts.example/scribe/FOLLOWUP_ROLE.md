# scribe/FOLLOWUP_ROLE

The brief for a second or final message to a business that was emailed once and
has not answered.

The distinction this prompt has to carry is that a follow-up is NOT another
attempt at the pitch: it puts the link back in front of someone who lost it,
asks one thing they can answer in seconds, and makes a plain no completely
painless. Repeating the offer is the failure mode, so the prompt names it.
