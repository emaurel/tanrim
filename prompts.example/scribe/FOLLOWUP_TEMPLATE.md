# scribe/FOLLOWUP_TEMPLATE

Two canonical follow-up shapes — the nudge and the final note — with the fixed
process facts and one adaptable slot each.

The parts that must not vary are the ones a recipient could be misled by: that
nothing is being withdrawn, that there is no deadline, that the price is the
one already quoted, and that saying no is invited and costs them nothing.
