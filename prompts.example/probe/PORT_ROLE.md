# probe/PORT_ROLE

The brief (and output contract) for reading a PORT client's existing website
into a dossier.

A port lead is a customer who asked for a rebuild, so this pass does not
qualify, score or judge their site — it extracts. The distinguishing fields are
`existing_site` (what they have) and `must_not_lose` (what the rebuild is
not allowed to drop).
