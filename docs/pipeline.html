What the code does that the plan doesn't say

Four things that fall out of reading the transitions rather than the intent.

  1. A failed build sends the lead back to research, not back to the builder

    When Forge finishes without producing index.html it calls advance_lead(lead_id, "qualified"). Because qualified routes to Probe's Dossier Desk, the sweep then re-runs the whole dossier, the photo read and the build. A missing file costs three agent runs, not one.

  2. Two rooms claim published, and the sweep picks Communications

    The Pitch Desk and The Outbox both declare it. role_for_stage("published") returns the first match in room order, which is echo — so an automatic dispatch goes to send the email, not write it. Scribe only ever gets a published lead through an explicit prefer_role="scribe", which today happens on exactly one path: you rejecting a pitch with a reason.

  3. A change request is not a special case, and that is deliberate

    When a business asks for something different, their words go into qa.problems and the lead returns to qa_failed — the identical path to you rejecting a build. It then walks the whole build, QA and publish loop again. The only thing that marks it is revision.requested_by == "client", which tells Forge the business has already seen this page (so change what they mentioned and leave the rest) and Scribe to send a two-line note rather than pitching again.

  4. Silence is a loss, on a timer

    A lead sitting at contacted for TANRIM_NO_REPLY_DAYS (21 by default) is moved to lost by the sweep. Most businesses never answer, and a board full of leads that are neither won nor lost buries the ones still worth chasing.

  5. The Watchtower is the only room you have to start yourself

    Nova's bench declares no stages, because sourcing takes a place rather than a lead. Every other room fires on its own the moment a lead reaches its stage.

Where it stops for you

Gate 1 · Shipping Bay

Raised by Courier when a lead reaches qa_passed. The card embeds the build at 390px from /staging/<lead_id>/. Approving deploys to Cloudflare Pages, checks domain availability and moves the lead to published. Rejecting merges your reason into qa.problems as a critical and sends it to qa_failed, which is where Forge reads its rebuild brief.

Gate 2 · Communications

Raised by Echo for a lead at published that has a draft, a recipient and a live preview. It refuses to raise at all while TANRIM_AGENCY_NAME or TANRIM_SENDER_EMAIL is unset, or while the draft contains billing language. Rejecting with a reason returns it to the Copy Desk to rewrite; rejecting silently marks it lost.

Gate 3 · Communications

Raised by Echo when you record that the business accepted. It carries the handover checklist — register the domain at OVH in their name, point it at Cloudflare, redeploy without the noindex and without the preview banner, send the files and the one-page doc. None of that is automated: registering a domain is irreversible and spends real money, so the system never holds a card. Approving the card is what marks the lead won.

Not a gate, but it stops too

Probe raises a thin_content card when a dossier comes back not_enough, and parks the lead back at qualified rather than building a page with nothing on it. That card is a notification — no approval reopens the path.
